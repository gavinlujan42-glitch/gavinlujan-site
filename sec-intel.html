PLATFORMS & STANDARDS

Clear roles, no category errors.

STIX structures intelligence and TAXII transports it. MISP and OpenCTI aggregate, enrich, correlate and share intelligence. They strengthen the pipeline; they are not automatically the original evidence.

SAFETY & CONFIDENCE

Useful without becoming dangerous.

Facts are separated from assessment. Claims retain provenance and timestamps. Indicators are defanged when appropriate. No live malware, weaponized exploit code, secrets, personal data or unredacted harmful content is published.
