DECISION RULE

Known exploited beats merely scary.

A reachable KEV on a critical asset should normally outrank a disconnected CVSS 10 finding with no credible exploitation path. EPSS helps sort the gray zone between those poles.

GOVERNANCE

Keep the evidence visible.

Do not collapse CVSS, KEV, EPSS and business criticality into an unexplained magic score. Preserve each signal, document exceptions and make residual exposure auditable.
