INTELLIGENCE DISCIPLINE

Fact ≠ estimate ≠ hypothesis.

VERIFIED

Benchmarks, filings, official announcements, permits and attributable disclosures.

ESTIMATED

Credible reporting or analyst estimates, always attributed and confidence-scored.

HYPOTHESIS

Possible undisclosed capacity inferred from observable signals. Never presented as fact.
