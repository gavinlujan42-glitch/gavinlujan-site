BLACK DIAMOND // NOIR RESPONSE UNIT

Incident Response.
Digital Forensics.

For complex, sensitive and consequential cyber incidents. Establish the facts. Contain the threat. Preserve the evidence. Give leadership a defensible path forward.

ENGAGED BY OBJECTIVE // DELIVERED BY OUTCOME

INCIDENT COMMAND

Triage, containment strategy, executive coordination and decision support during active incidents.

DIGITAL FORENSICS

Forensic acquisition, artifact analysis, timeline reconstruction and evidence-focused technical findings.

COMPROMISE ASSESSMENT

Endpoint, identity, cloud and network investigation focused on determining scope and persistence.

EVIDENCE PRESERVATION

Documented collection and preservation practices designed to support defensible internal, insurance, legal and regulatory processes.

RECOVERY + HARDENING

Eradication priorities, recovery validation, control improvements and post-incident lessons translated into action.

EXECUTIVE FINDINGS

Technical evidence converted into concise findings, uncertainty statements, decisions and prioritized next objectives.

OBJECTIVE-BASED RESPONSE MODEL

OBJECTIVE → SCOPE → CONTAIN → INVESTIGATE → EVIDENCE → FINDINGS → HARDEN

Black Diamond engagements are scoped around a defined mission objective and deliverable rather than open-ended activity. Scope, assumptions, authorization, evidence-handling requirements and completion criteria are established before execution.

Black Diamond provides authorized defensive cybersecurity, incident-response and forensic services. Engagement scope must be documented and authorized by the system or data owner. Legal conclusions, e-discovery requirements and law-enforcement evidence procedures should be coordinated with qualified counsel and appropriate authorities.
