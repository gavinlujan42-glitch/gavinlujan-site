A service operating model that turns fragmented security signals into mission-aware priorities, governed remediation, executive evidence, and continuously improving cyber posture. Human authority remains explicit at consequential decision points.

Service Spine

DISCOVER → NORMALIZE → ENRICH → PRIORITIZE → REMEDIATE → VALIDATE → REPORT → GOVERN
Attack Surface + Asset Assurance

Build a mission-aware inventory across infrastructure, cloud, applications, identity, data, OT/IoT and suppliers.

Risk-Based Vulnerability Management

Correlate scanner, threat, exploitability, asset criticality and control evidence to focus scarce remediation capacity.

Application Security

Connect code, dependency, API, configuration and runtime findings to release gates and accountable owners.

Control + Compliance Assurance

Map operational evidence to NIST CSF 2.0, RMF, CIS, PCI DSS and organizational policy without turning compliance into checkbox theater.

Incident + Resilience Readiness

Test detection, escalation, recovery, immutable evidence and continuity assumptions before an incident does it for you.

Executive Cyber Risk

Translate technical exposure into mission consequence, trends, SLA performance, exceptions and decisions requiring leadership.

Operating principles

Integrate existing tools before buying more. Deduplicate noise. Score with context. Assign owners and SLAs. Validate remediation rather than trusting ticket closure. Preserve historical posture. Keep scoring explainable. Require human approval for risk acceptance, disruptive remediation, privileged access changes and external reporting.

Design inspiration: contemporary risk-based vulnerability management patterns including unified asset context, prioritization, remediation orchestration, validation and trend reporting. This is an independent Gavin Lujan operating model, not a TAC Security product or affiliation.
