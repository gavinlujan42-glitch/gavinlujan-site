NIST RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

Security is not a product.
It is a life cycle.

The RMF turns risk into a disciplined executive process, connecting mission context, system impact, controls, evidence, authorization, and continuous monitoring.

RMF SP 800-37
  1. 01 PREPARE Set context and priorities
  2. 02 CATEGORIZE Determine system impact
  3. 03 SELECT Choose risk-based controls
  4. 04 IMPLEMENT Deploy and document
  5. 05 ASSESS Test control effectiveness
  6. 06 AUTHORIZE Make the risk decision
  7. 07 MONITOR Maintain continuous awareness
