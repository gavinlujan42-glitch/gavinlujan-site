## Campaign post 2: The objective
An incident-response engagement should begin with a question worth answering.

Was privileged identity compromised? What systems were affected? Is persistence still present? Can recovery be trusted? What evidence supports the conclusion?

BLACK DIAMOND starts with the objective and works backward to the evidence required to answer it.

## Campaign post 3: Evidence
Speed matters during an incident. So does preserving the ability to explain what happened later.

BLACK DIAMOND treats evidence preservation, provenance, timelines and uncertainty as first-class response requirements.

Facts first. Conclusions second. Decisions supported by both.

## Campaign post 4: Executive command
The SOC sees alerts. Leadership sees mission risk.

BLACK DIAMOND connects the two: technical investigation translated into scope, confidence, decisions, recovery priorities and defensible executive findings.

## Campaign post 5: Human-accountable AI
AI can accelerate forensic search, correlation and hypothesis generation. It should not silently become the investigator of record.

BLACK DIAMOND uses automation as force multiplication while preserving accountable human judgment for evidence handling, material findings and final conclusions.

## Brand direction
Obsidian and graphite field. Restrained cold cyan telemetry. Brushed silver diamond geometry. Minimal gold reserved for executive authority or verified findings. Evidence-line and timeline motifs rather than generic hacker imagery. The visual tone is special investigations laboratory, not Hollywood cyberpunk.

## Governance note
Services are defensive and require documented authorization from the relevant system/data owner. Engagements involving litigation, criminal investigation, formal e-discovery or regulatory evidence requirements should coordinate procedures with qualified counsel and appropriate authorities.
