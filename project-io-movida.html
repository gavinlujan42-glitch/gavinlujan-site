La Movida
in the Age of AI.

A public-integrity intelligence prototype for tracing how money, authority, relationships, procurement, regulation and measurable public outcomes move through New Mexico government. It does not decide guilt. It makes the public record legible.

A relationship is not wrongdoing. A pattern is not proof. An allegation is not a finding. Every public dollar, however, should have a traceable lineage from appropriation to measurable public benefit.

CONTESTED / PENDING

Cross-agency test of water, environmental permitting, lobbying, economic development, administrative process and judicial review.
