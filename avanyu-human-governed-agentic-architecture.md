# Human-Governed Agentic Architecture

**Architecture doctrine · 12 September 2026**

## Operating principle

AI supplies velocity. People retain authority.

Avanyu uses bounded specialist agents to multiply research, engineering, security, compliance and operational capacity. Consequential authority remains human-owned. Every client action is constrained by contract, scope, policy, identity, evidence and an accountable human approval boundary.

## Control plane

**Client → Contract → Scope → Policy → Identity → Agent → Evidence → Human Authority → Client**

This is the governing information flow for Avanyu AI Architects, Avanyu Cloud, ZIAWOLF, ZIATec and associated cyber technology systems.

## Agent roster

- **SOC-01 · Picarus Sentinel** — defensive telemetry, threat modeling, incident playbooks and bounded remediation.
- **DEV-01 · Avanyu Core** — architecture, application engineering and infrastructure-as-code generation.
- **SEC-02 · NIST / PCI Auditor** — evidence mapping, gap analysis, POA&M development and compliance support.
- **OPS-01 · Platform Engineering** — Terraform, containers, platform engineering, observability and change plans.
- **COM-01 · ZIAWOLF Media** — client-facing artifacts, visualization and communications under human review.

## Authorization tiers

| Tier | Authority | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Observe | Read telemetry and evidence only. |
| 1 | Recommend | Produce findings and proposed actions. |
| 2 | Generate | Produce code, policy, configuration or change artifacts without execution. |
| 3 | Human-approved execution | Execute only after an authorized human approves the specific change. |
| 4 | Pre-authorized autonomous remediation | Limited to explicitly defined, reversible, low-blast-radius actions with logging, rollback and policy constraints. |

No agent receives unlimited standing authority. Privilege is task-bound, time-bound and evidence-producing.

## Picarus directive

Picarus is a privileged defensive specialist, not the control-plane authority. Missing immutable recovery evidence elevates infrastructure risk. Remediation recommendations must be driven by empirical telemetry and designed to minimize mission disruption. Automated execution is permitted only within the applicable authorization tier. Every consequential action must generate a plain-English, machine-readable audit trail suitable for governance and compliance review.

## Evidence plane

Agent prompts, context retrieval, tool calls, proposed changes, approvals, execution results, rollback events and compliance evidence should be captured in tamper-resistant logging. RAG/vector knowledge is treated as context, not authority. Retrieved material must retain provenance wherever practicable.

## Commercial model

Professional services are sold as defensible service units such as **Advisory Hours, Architecture Sprints, Assessments, Managed Services and Fixed Deliverables**. Cloud, model and infrastructure consumption is separately measured and may be included in a service price or transparently passed through. AI processing time is not represented as equivalent to human professional-service hours.

## Advisory-first boundary

Engagements begin with explicit scope and authority. Architecture, assessment and advisory artifacts do not silently confer operational control. Client responsibilities, execution authority, change approval and acceptance criteria belong in the governing agreement and statement of work.

## Enterprise promise

**Enterprise architecture without enterprise overhead.** Human-accountable AI agents perform bounded research, engineering, security, compliance and operational analysis under a unified evidence and authorization plane.

The objective is not autonomous technology for its own sake. It is faster, more legible and more resilient mission delivery with a human being unmistakably accountable for consequential decisions.
