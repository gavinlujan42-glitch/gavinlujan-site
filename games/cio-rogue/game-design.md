# CIO Rogue — Game Design v0.2

## Player character: The CIO
Core attributes use a D&D-style 3–18 scale.

- **INT — Architecture:** systems reasoning, technology choices, incident analysis.
- **WIS — Judgment:** risk, prioritization, governance, detecting bad ideas wearing expensive suits.
- **CHA — Executive Presence:** board, CEO, vendors, recruiting, budget defense.
- **CON — Resilience:** crisis stamina and resistance to burnout.
- **DEX — Agility:** delivery response and organizational maneuvering.
- **STR — Authority:** ability to force a hard decision when consensus fails.

Derived resources: Security, Reliability, Delivery, Morale, Reputation, Budget, Technical Debt, Executive Trust.

## Core check
`d20 + attribute modifier + role bonus + situational modifiers >= difficulty class`

Difficulty classes:
- 8 routine
- 10 normal
- 12 tricky
- 15 hard
- 18 severe
- 22 legendary enterprise nonsense

Natural 20: critical success. Natural 1: complication.

## Encounter round
1. Signal arrives.
2. CIO assesses.
3. Choose lead party member.
4. Choose management action.
5. Roll/check outcome.
6. Apply operational consequence.
7. Advance threat clocks and technical debt.
8. Record decision in Chronicle.

## Ethical design
Security gameplay represents defensive, authorized enterprise work. Offensive-looking fantasy actions resolve into safe abstractions such as containment, validation, patching, investigation and recovery rather than real intrusion instructions.
