Watching Jupiter from Orbit.

Published September 10, 2026 · 8:49 AM MDT

ORANGE · UNCHANGED

The material change is an energy-development signal: Oracle says it has issued an RFP for 2 GW of new renewable generation in New Mexico. County governance also slipped two weeks when monitoring-committee appointments were deferred to September 22. No evidence surfaced establishing corruption, and no new Supreme Court merits ruling was located by publication time.

Verified facts

Oracle renewable-energy RFP

Oracle announced on September 8 that it is seeking proposals for 2 GW of new renewable generation located in New Mexico. The solicitation covers solar, wind, geothermal and other renewable technologies, prioritizing projects deliverable between 2027 and 2031. Oracle ties the effort to a goal of matching Jupiter’s energy use with 100% carbon-free energy by 2031.

This is evidence of a solicitation, not evidence that projects have been selected, financed, interconnected, built or contracted. Oracle’s public release does not identify a proposal deadline, interconnection points, storage requirement, transmission plan, procurement form or executed power-purchase agreement.

Monitoring committee delayed

County commissioners did not appoint the 11-member Project Jupiter Community Monitoring Committee at the September 8 meeting. Reporting on the meeting says the decision was moved to September 22. The delay leaves the committee without a verified public roster while construction and regulatory disputes continue.

Known project dependencies

Oracle expressly conditions its employment, revenue, investment and economic-impact projections on approval of the air permit and pipeline as originally planned. The proposed Bloom fuel-cell system remains dependent on natural gas even if Oracle later matches consumption with renewable generation on an annual accounting basis.

Allegations and disputed positions

NMDOJ continues to support challengers in the water and air matters, arguing that OSE and NMED departed from required process. OSE, NMED and project entities dispute that characterization. These are litigated legal positions, not findings by the Supreme Court.

Opponent claims about preferential treatment, water harm and inadequate review remain allegations unless and until supported by agency findings, court rulings or verified records. Nothing reviewed today supports a corruption inference.

Analytical judgment

The RFP separates powering the site from matching its consumption. Jupiter’s onsite Bloom system is designed for gas-fed, always-on generation. New renewable projects may offset or match that demand over time, but the release does not show that 2 GW of renewables will physically serve the campus at every hour.

The committee delay is a modest but real governance gap. Its importance depends on what happens September 22: who is appointed, what affiliations are disclosed, what expertise is represented, and whether the committee receives underlying data rather than curated summaries.

Material change from the September 9 brief: the renewable announcement is now documented as an RFP with a 2-GW target and 2027–2031 delivery window; the expected September 8 committee appointments did not occur.

  • Does “100% carbon-free energy matching” mean hourly matching, annual matching, renewable-energy certificates, direct PPAs or another structure?
  • Where are the RFP instructions, bid deadline, delivery nodes, interconnection requirements and evaluation weights?
