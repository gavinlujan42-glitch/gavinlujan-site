HPC / AI SECURITY

Threat surface

SEV 1 · MODEL + RESEARCH ASSET PROTECTION

Protect model weights, training data, schedulers, storage and privileged management planes with strong identity, segmentation, encryption, provenance and auditable administrative access.

SEV 2 · SUPPLY CHAIN + ACCELERATOR STACK

Maintain hardware/software inventories, signed artifacts, controlled driver and firmware lifecycles, dependency governance and rapid vendor remediation paths.

SEV 2 · MULTI-TENANT / SCHEDULER BOUNDARIES

Harden workload isolation, scheduler permissions, secrets handling, service accounts and east-west telemetry. Apply the NIST HPC security overlay as a governance baseline.

SEV 2 · AI DATA CENTER CONTROL PLANE

Treat orchestration, identity, storage, networking and physical infrastructure as one security system. Map controls across training, inference and application workflows.
