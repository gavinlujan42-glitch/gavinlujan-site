What we can discuss—but not promote to fact

  • Announced campuses: power reservations and financing do not prove energized racks.
  • Model-linked compute: model performance cannot reliably back-calculate cluster size.

Evidence rule: corroborated plans remain plans. Promote operational status only with dated owner/operator evidence of commissioning; label measured benchmark results separately. Two reports repeating a capacity claim do not establish energized compute.
