RESEARCH RADAR · NEXT VALIDATION TARGETS

What the program needs to prove next

RESOURCE INVENTORY

Validate compute, accelerator, storage, network, scheduler, eligibility, utilization and lifecycle data with each institution.

GOVERNANCE MAP

Document ownership, data classifications, research agreements, security boundaries and the authorities that cannot be federated.

FUNDING RADAR

Track NSF, DOE and state opportunities around cyberinfrastructure, AI, workforce, water, climate and regional innovation.

WORKLOAD PIPELINE

Identify real research workloads that can graduate from notebooks to university HPC and national research infrastructure.

WATER MVP

Define one bounded basin or hydrologic question, authoritative datasets, model chain, uncertainty measures and measurable outcome.

WORKFORCE

Design a progression through Linux, Python, containers, Slurm, GPU, MPI, AI, security and scientific reproducibility.

RESEARCH POSITION

The moat is the network.

Hardware can be purchased. The harder asset to reproduce is the network of scientists, students, national laboratories, universities, public institutions, companies, data, governance and pathways to appropriate compute.
