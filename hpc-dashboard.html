RESEARCH POSITION

The moat is the network.

Hardware can be purchased. The harder asset to reproduce is the network of scientists, students, national laboratories, universities, public institutions, companies, data, governance and pathways to appropriate compute. The dashboard tracks that thesis as a living research program.
